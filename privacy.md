# Little Blooms Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 30 September 2026
**Data controller:** Little Blooms, Jewar Compound, Sheikh Zayed, Egypt

**Privacy contact:** littleblooms30@gmail.com

This policy explains how Little Blooms uses the LittleSteps nursery app. It is intended for parents and guardians, staff, and administrators. Please contact us if you have a question about a child's information.

## Information we use

- Account and access details: name, email address, role, account status, class and child assignments, and sign-in records.
- Child and care details: child name, class, daily food, water and bathroom updates, teacher notes, announcements, schedules, menus, and allergy notes entered by nursery staff.
- Pickup details: authorized collector name, type and optional phone number; time, staff confirmation, and a short-lived QR pickup token or its hash.
- Requests and accountability records: access and deletion requests, review decisions, and audit events about changes or account activity.
- Push delivery details: device notification token, child name and event type in a queued alert, and delivery status. A child's name can appear on the device lock screen if notifications are enabled; the alert does not preview care notes or other changed content.

The camera is used to scan pickup QR codes; the app does not need to store a photograph of the code. The app may keep sign-in state on the device.

## Why we use it

We use this information to provide nursery care, show guardians records for their children, let assigned staff update care information, verify authorized pickup, send relevant alerts, and handle support and privacy requests. Access in the app is limited by account role and child or class assignment. We do not use the app to sell personal data or show advertising.

## Service providers and transfers

Supabase provides authentication and database services. Firebase Cloud Messaging transports push notifications to registered devices. These providers may process information outside Egypt according to their service configuration and terms. We share only information needed to operate those services. Nursery staff with an assigned role can access records needed for their work. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect a child or another person's safety.

## How long we keep information

- Account and child-care records: while the child is enrolled, then reviewed for deletion or anonymization within 12 months after enrollment ends.
- Pickup confirmations: 24 months after the pickup. QR token records: 30 days after expiry.
- Access, deletion, and related privacy request records: 24 months after the request is closed.
- Audit events: 24 months after the event.
- Push delivery records: 90 days after creation. Device notification tokens: while the device is registered to an active account, then removed when no longer needed.

An active legal claim, safety investigation, or legal duty may require a particular record to be kept longer. We restrict access to records under such a hold, review the hold periodically, and delete or anonymize the record when the reason ends. Provider backups may retain deleted data temporarily under the provider's backup cycle; restored data is subject to the same cleanup review. These periods are Little Blooms' operating targets, not a statement that an app account deletion request immediately erases every record.

## Your choices and requests

A parent or legal guardian can request access, correction, export, or deletion by emailing littleblooms30@gmail.com. A signed-in user can also submit an account deletion request in the app. You can contact us by email even if you cannot sign in. We verify the requester's identity and authority, review any records that must be kept for safety or legal reasons, and explain the outcome. Submitting a request does not automatically delete an account or its records. You may also raise a complaint with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Center.

You can disable push notifications in your device settings. This does not stop essential nursery records from being kept under the periods above.

## Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its date when our practices materially change. Email littleblooms30@gmail.com for privacy questions. Messages can be sent at any time; replies may not be immediate.
