# Little Blooms App Terms

**Last updated:** 30 September 2026
**Provider:** Little Blooms, Jewar Compound, Sheikh Zayed, Egypt

**Contact:** littleblooms30@gmail.com

LittleSteps is Little Blooms' account-based app for authorized parents or guardians, teachers, and administrators. It shows care updates, schedules and menus, and supports pickup verification. These app terms cover access to the app; any applicable nursery enrollment agreement, fees, and care policies continue to apply separately.

## Accounts and access

The nursery decides who may use the app and which child or class records each account may access. Users should provide accurate account information, protect their sign-in credentials, sign out of shared devices, and tell the nursery promptly if an account or phone is lost or used without permission. Users must not try to access records outside their assigned role or share pickup QR codes with an unauthorized person.

## Care information and pickup

The app is a communication and operational tool. Staff should enter accurate care and pickup information. For immediate care or pickup matters, speak directly with nursery staff; an app message or push notification is not an emergency service. Pickup remains subject to the nursery's identity and authorization checks.

## Availability and changes

Network, device, or service problems can delay updates and notifications. The nursery may correct records, change app features, or suspend an account when needed to protect children, users, or the service. The nursery will communicate material changes to these terms and update the effective date.

## Privacy and support

The Little Blooms Privacy Policy, available in the app, explains how personal information is handled. For app access, corrections, privacy requests, or support, contact littleblooms30@gmail.com.
