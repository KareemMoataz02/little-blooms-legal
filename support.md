# Little Blooms Support

**Nursery:** Little Blooms

**Email:** littleblooms30@gmail.com

**Business address:** Jewar Compound, Sheikh Zayed, Egypt

**Email availability:** Messages may be sent 24/7; replies may not be immediate

For sign-in problems, app errors, account access, care-record corrections, or privacy requests, email the address above. Include your name, the account email, a short description of the issue, and the time it happened. Do not send passwords, pickup QR codes, or children's private records by email.

For an urgent care or pickup concern, speak directly with nursery staff. Email and push alerts should not be relied on as emergency channels.

## Request account or data deletion

Email littleblooms30@gmail.com with the subject “LittleSteps account deletion request” and the email address used for the account. A signed-in user can also submit a deletion request in the app. We will verify the requester's identity and authority, review the request, and explain the outcome. Some pickup, request, or audit records may be kept for the periods described in the Little Blooms Privacy Policy when needed for safety or legal reasons. You can use this email route without reinstalling or signing in to the app.
